Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

WALNOR respects your privacy and processes personal data only to the extent necessary for communication, project inquiries and engineering cooperation. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when using this website and contact form.

1. Data Controller

The controller of personal data is WALNOR Engineering. Contact details and identification information will be provided before entering any contractual relationship or project cooperation.

2. What Data Is Collected

WALNOR only collects personal data voluntarily submitted through the contact form or direct communication. This may include:

  • Name and surname
  • Email address
  • Company name
  • Technical information related to your inquiry or project
  • Communication details necessary for engineering cooperation

Personal data is processed solely for communication purposes, preparation of engineering proposals, technical consultation and potential contractual cooperation.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Personal data is processed on the basis of legitimate interest and pre-contractual communication according to Article 6(1)(b) and Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

4. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for the period necessary to process inquiries, maintain communication and fulfill legal obligations. Technical communication related to engineering cooperation may be archived for reasonable business protection purposes.

5. Data Sharing and Security

WALNOR does not sell, rent or distribute personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect submitted data against unauthorized access, misuse or loss.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR regulations, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Object to or restrict data processing
  • Request information about how data is processed

Requests related to personal data protection may be submitted through the contact form available on this website.

7. Contact

If you have questions regarding personal data processing or privacy protection, please use the contact form available on the Contact page.
