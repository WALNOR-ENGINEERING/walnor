Cyclone Separator Flow Optimization

Evaluation of multi-phase flow dynamics and particle separation efficiency inside an industrial cyclone separator. Using advanced turbulence modeling, we analyzed pressure drop characteristics and velocity fields to optimize the internal geometric parameters.

Methodology

Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), Reynolds Stress Model (RSM), Discrete Phase Modeling (DPM) for particle tracking.

Objectives

  • Minimize total pressure drop across the system.
  • Maximize separation efficiency for particles under 5 micrometers.
  • Eliminate short-circuiting fluid pathways near the vortex finder.

Engineering Outcomes

The modified vortex finder length and optimized inlet section geometry led to a 14% reduction in pressure drop while maintaining the required separation cut-off size, directly translating to lower fan energy consumption.

Rotating Flow & Impeller Aerodynamics

Aerodynamic analysis of an industrial fan stage designed for heavy-duty gas recirculation. The study focuses on transient blade loading, boundary layer separation on blade surfaces, and the interaction between the rotating impeller and stationary stator vanes.

Methodology

Transient CFD, Moving Reference Frame (MRF) for steady-state initialization, Sliding Mesh approach for transient validation, SST k-omega turbulence model.

Objectives

  • Identify localized stall zones and aerodynamic backflow regimes.
  • Evaluate pressure fluctuations causing structural micro-vibrations.
  • Optimize the blade angle distribution for variable operational speed.

Engineering Outcomes

Identified a critical flow separation bubble on the suction side of the blade at low flow rates. Redesigning the blade camber profile eliminated the stall zone, expanding the stable operational margin of the fan by 18%.

Thermal Cooling & Conjugate Heat Transfer

Conjugate Heat Transfer (CHT) analysis simulating the simultaneous conduction within solid electronic components and forced convection cooling from surrounding fluid channels. Designed to resolve thermal stress accumulation in high-power industrial inverter assemblies.

Methodology

Conjugate Heat Transfer (CHT), Coupled Thermal-Fluid Solver, Volumetric Heat Source mapping based on operational electrical losses.

Objectives

  • Locate hidden thermal hotspots under peak load conditions.
  • Optimize heatsink geometry (fin density and thickness).
  • Ensure uniform airflow distribution across all parallel cooling tracks.

Engineering Outcomes

Revealed a flow stagnation zone behind the primary capacitor bank. By implementing air-guiding baffles and optimizing the heatsink fin spacing, maximum junction temperature was lowered by 22°C, significantly increasing component lifespan.

Multiphase Flow & Free Surface Dynamics

Transient simulation of multi-phase liquid-gas interaction inside high-speed industrial filling systems. The analysis captures free-surface deformations, liquid splashing patterns, and potential air entrainment that could compromise product quality.

Methodology

Transient Multiphase Flow, Volume of Fluid (VOF) model, Geo-Reconstruct interface tracking scheme, dynamic mesh refinement.

Objectives

  • Map free surface profile variations during rapid filling cycles.
  • Predict high-shear zones capable of causing fluid aeration or foaming.
  • Validate structural integrity of tank internal baffles against fluid impact.

Engineering Outcomes

Quantified the exact timing and location of air entrapment loops during the injection phase. Modifying the nozzle submersion depth and filling speed curve suppressed interface turbulence, reducing air entrainment close to zero.
