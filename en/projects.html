Selected Work

Cyclone Separator Flow Optimization

Analysis of airflow behavior, turbulence reduction and efficiency improvement.

Show detail

Problem

High turbulence caused significant energy losses and local erosion of the separator walls, reducing overall operational life cycle.

Solution

Advanced CFD simulation of internal gas-solid phase interactions. Geometry optimization of the inlet nozzle reduced vortex formation.

Result

−18% pressure loss, +12% separation efficiency verified by subsequent physical testing.

Thermal Distribution Study

Evaluation of heat transfer, cooling efficiency and thermal stability.

Show detail

Problem

Uneven cooling rates causing severe thermal stress and premature micro-cracking in sensitive mechanical components.

Solution

Transient thermal analysis to evaluate heat flux. Redesign of cooling channels and optimization of fluid delivery lines.

Result

Improved thermal stability by 25% and completely eliminated critical localized hot-spots.

Rotating Flow Analysis

Investigation of airflow around rotating components and aerodynamic performance.

Show detail

Problem

Excessive aerodynamic drag around high-speed rotating parts, reducing mechanical transmission efficiency.

Solution

MRF (Moving Reference Frame) simulation to model rotational forces and modification of component housing geometry.

Result

Aerodynamic drag reduced by 8%, leading to direct savings in engine power consumption.

Multiphase Particle Transport

Simulation of particle flow, dust behavior and separation efficiency.

Show detail

Problem

High accumulation of dust particles in ducting dead zones, presenting an operational hazard and causing clogging.

Solution

DPM (Discrete Phase Model) track simulation to trace particle trajectories and implementation of auxiliary air jets.

Result

95% dust reduction in critical areas, drastically lowering the maintenance window requirements.
